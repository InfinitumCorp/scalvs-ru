Chrome extension privacy policy

The Infinitum Corporation. Last updated 8 November 2026.

What the extension sends

When you press Send in the side panel, the extension sends your question to the SCALVS.ai service, by default scalvs.app, or another SCALVS.ai address you set in Options. Along with the question it may send:

  • text you selected, when you use Ask SCALVS.ai about the selection;
  • the visible text of the current page (up to 5,000 characters), its title and address, when you use Ask SCALVS.ai about this page or Add this page;
  • the previous answer in the conversation, so follow-up questions have context.

The page is read only at the moment you choose one of those actions. Nothing is sent until you press Send. The service answers with an AI model and streams the reply back to the panel.

What it stores on your device

  • The SCALVS.ai address you set in Options, in Chrome's local extension storage. Reset it in Options or remove the extension to delete it.
  • An unsent draft (question and attached text), in session storage, which Chrome clears when the browser closes.

The conversation itself is kept only in the open side panel and is gone when you close it or press New chat.

What it does not do

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no browsing history, no remote code, and no API keys in the extension. We do not sell your data or share it with anyone other than the SCALVS.ai service you send it to. Data handling follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
